🎬 CRIME SCENE: THE INTERROGATION

A Short Drama Script for 3 Characters

SCENE: Police Interrogation Room — Night
🕵️ Detective Ahasanul
Good evening, Mr. Karim. Thank you for coming. Please sit down. Can you tell me what happened last night?
👤 Mr. Karim
Good evening, sir. Yes, I can. It was very scary.
👮 Officer Rahman
Don't worry, sir. You should relax. We want to help you. Would you like some water?
👤 Mr. Karim
No, thank you. I am okay. Well, it was about 9 PM. I was closing my shop. Then I heard a loud sound from the back.
🕵️ Detective Ahasanul
Where were you at that time?
👤 Mr. Karim
I was near the cash box. I was counting money. I looked outside. I could see two men running from the jewelry shop next to my shop.
👮 Officer Rahman
Good. Could you tell us about their looks?
👤 Mr. Karim
One man was very tall. He wore a black jacket and a red cap. The other man was short. But I could not see his face well.
🕵️ Detective Ahasanul
Okay. Did you see anything strange in their actions?
👤 Mr. Karim
Yes! The tall man had a big bag. It must be the stolen things. The alarm of the jewelry shop was ringing very loud.
👮 Officer Rahman
You must be careful now, sir. If you see something bad, you should call us fast.
👤 Mr. Karim
I will, sir. But I feel bad. If I had called the police before, they might not run away so easily.
🕵️ Detective Ahasanul
Don't feel bad, Mr. Karim. You did the right thing. You did not fight with them. It could be very dangerous.
👮 Officer Rahman
Sir, would you know them if you see them again?
👤 Mr. Karim
Yes, I think so. If I see that tall man again, I would know him. His red cap was very easy to see.
🕵️ Detective Ahasanul
Very good. What time did you say it was?
👤 Mr. Karim
It might be around 9:15 PM. I am not sure. I wanted to go home fast.
👮 Officer Rahman
That's fine. Can you remember if they used a car?
👤 Mr. Karim
Oh yes! I heard a car start after they ran away. It was a dark car. But I did not see the number.
🕵️ Detective Ahasanul
Good. Every small thing helps. Mr. Karim, we may call you again. You may need to see the men in a line. Would that be okay?
👤 Mr. Karim
Yes, of course. I will help you catch them.
👮 Officer Rahman
Thank you, sir. If you remember more things, please call this number.
🕵️ Detective Ahasanul
Yes. And you must not go near bad people alone. Your safety is important.
👤 Mr. Karim
I understand. Thank you, officers.

🎭 Acting Tips Summary

  • Detective Ahasanul: গম্ভীর, ধীরে ধীরে, প্রশ্ন করার সময় নোটবুকে লিখবেন.
  • Officer Rahman: বন্ধুত্বপূর্ণ, হাসিমুখে, সাহায্য করতে চায় এমন ভঙ্গি
  • Mr. Karim: একটু ভয় পাওয়া, কাঁপা কাঁপা গলা, হাত নাড়ানো

📝 Acting Notes

  • Speak slowly and deeply (গম্ভীর স্বর)
  • Take notes in a notebook while asking questions
  • Maintain eye contact — authoritative but calm
  • Occasionally pause before asking the next question
  • Reassure Mr. Karim when he feels guilty

📝 Acting Notes

  • Smile warmly — be the friendly face
  • Offer water and comfort to Mr. Karim
  • Show genuine concern for witness safety
  • Use encouraging tone: "Don't worry, sir"
  • Ask follow-up details about suspects

  • Look down when feeling guilty about not calling earlier
  • Be cooperative but clearly shaken by the experience
